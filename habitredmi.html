1. Information Collection and Use

To provide a seamless experience across multiple devices, Привычки Pro ("we", "our", or "the App") collects several different types of information for various purposes:

  • Personal Authentication Data: When you register or log in using Email or SMS, we collect your email address or phone number. This data is used solely to identify your account and secure your access.
  • User-Generated Data (Cloud Sync): Your habits, daily progress, reminders, and financial records (income/expenses) are uploaded to and stored in Google Firebase. This enables real-time synchronization, allowing you to access your latest data from any device by logging into your account.
  • Device and Usage Information: We may collect basic information about your mobile device, such as model, OS version, and unique device identifiers to optimize app performance.
2. Service Providers and Third-Party Data

We employ third-party companies to facilitate our Service and provide the Service on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Database & Auth): We use Firebase for secure cloud storage and user authentication. Firebase handles your data with high-level encryption both during transit and at rest.
  • Google AdMob (Advertising): The App uses AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your Advertising ID to personalize ads and analyze ad performance.
  • Google Play Services: Used for app stability, updates, and integrated features.
3. Data Storage and Security

Cloud Security: By using Firebase, your data is protected by Google's industry-leading security infrastructure.

Local Storage: For your convenience, a cached version of your data may be stored locally on your device to allow offline access.

User Control: You have full control over your data. You can edit or delete your habit and financial records at any time within the App.

We implement strict security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information.
4. Data Export and Portability

We believe you should own your data. Привычки Pro provides built-in tools for:

PDF Export – Generate detailed progress reports CSV Export – Excel-compatible files for backup/analysis

You can export your complete habit history and financial ledger at any time without any restrictions.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time as we introduce new features like advanced analytics or social sharing. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Чтобы обеспечить бесперебойную работу на нескольких устройствах, Привычки Pro ("мы", "наш" или "Приложение") собирает несколько различных типов информации для различных целей:
